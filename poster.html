War in Ukraine · Lead story

Ukraine brings down part of Putin’s Kerch Bridge

Ukrainian missiles and drones struck the 12-mile bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea for a second straight night. A road span fell into the strait; Russia confirmed the damage.

President Zelenskyy hailed the attack as “unprecedented” — the biggest blow yet to Moscow’s signature supply route.

Reported 17 Nov · BBC · The Guardian · Reuters
Middle East · Gulf on edge

US and Iran trade fire as Washington strikes Qeshm Island

US jets hit an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island after Iran fired drones at a US radar base in Bahrain — its third attack there in five days. Trump said the US is “looking into” reports of an Iranian dirty-bomb plot; Tehran denies it.

Technology · Markets

Nvidia posts record $115bn quarter — but won’t bring back Huang

The AI chip giant doubled revenue year on year, yet said ousted chief Jensen Huang will not return — days after a spy scandal rattled the firm.

France · Strikes

146,000 march across France over the cost of living

Unions called a second nationwide strike for higher wages; 28,000 marched in Paris as the protest movement eyes the 2027 election.

Diplomacy · Asia

Trump heads to Shenzhen for APEC talks with Xi and Putin

The US president flew to Asia for a rare three-way meeting on 19 November — and raised a possible meeting with North Korea’s Kim Jong Un.

Health · United States

US loses measles-free status after 26 years

More than 5,400 cases and 10 deaths this year — the worst outbreak since the early 1990s — cost America its elimination status.
